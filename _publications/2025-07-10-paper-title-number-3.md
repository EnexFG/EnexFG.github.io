<p align="justify"> <b>Abstract.</b> This paper develops a High-Frequency Economic Index (HFEI) for Ecuador using a Bayesian dynamic factor model estimated with weekly, monthly, and quarterly official indicators. A pseudo-week structure harmonizes the timing of observations and their aggregation across frequencies. We compare twelve specifications that differ in their factor-loading dynamics and volatility assumptions, selecting the model with the lowest Deviance Information Criterion. We use the selected model to characterize historical economic conditions and estimate pseudo-weekly recession probabilities through a time-varying mean regime-switching model. In a pseudo-real-time evaluation over 2020–2024, the selected model reduces GDP forecast RMSE by 30–35% relative to an autoregressive benchmark. The framework integrates dispersed and asynchronous official statistics into a common measure of economic activity in a setting with limited timely information. </p>
